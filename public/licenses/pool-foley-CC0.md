# Pool footstep recordings

Author: Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank.

Source recordings, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:

- 0179, slippers on a tiled floor: https://bigsoundbank.com/steps-slipper-on-tiling-s0179.html
- 0690, shoes in water: https://bigsoundbank.com/shoes-in-the-water-s0690.html

License deed: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Full legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

This project uses sixteen edited single-step excerpts, converted to mono 32 kHz PCM, high-pass filtered at 65 Hz, faded and peak-normalized. Playback applies room reverberation, gain and low-pass filtering. These edits are by the pool project; the original recordings are credited to the author above.
